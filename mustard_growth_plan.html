Strategy Overview
Three pillars. One compounding engine.
🏪

Convert the base

Move existing offline customers online. The most trusted, highest-converting audience you'll ever reach — and they're already yours.

MONTHS 1–2
📈

Capture new demand

SEO, Google Shopping, and paid social — reaching buyers who are actively searching for ethnic wear, especially around India's festival calendar.

MONTHS 2–4
♻️

Retain and grow

Loyalty, email, WhatsApp, referrals. Turn one-time buyers into a predictable recurring revenue base. The flywheel that compounds.

MONTHS 4–6+
Each stage builds on the last. No wasted spend — every rupee goes in only after the previous layer is converting.
Days 1–7 · Foundations
Zero spend. Maximum signal.
  • 1
    Full GA4 funnel auditFind top exit pages, best-performing products, current conversion rate and average order value
  • 2
    Heatmap setup (Hotjar free)Homepage + top 3 product pages — see exactly where users drop off
  • 3
    Identify top 20 productsBy revenue — all early optimization effort focuses here first, not the full catalog
  • 4
    Abandoned cart auditIs recovery even set up? If not, this is the single highest-ROI fix on the site
  • 5
    Install GTM + Meta PixelNo ads yet — just data collection. Pixel needs 60–90 days of data before retargeting works well
Amazon and Myntra obsess over funnel data before adding traffic. Find where money is leaking before spending a rupee on ads.
Week 1–2 · Plug the Leaks
Revenue from traffic you already have.
  • 1
    Abandoned cart recovery3-email sequence: 1hr / 24hr / 72hr + WhatsApp message. Typical 8–15% recovery rate
  • 2
    Top 20 product page rewriteAI-optimised titles, descriptions, size guides. Better sequencing of images (lifestyle → detail → fabric)
  • 3
    Post-purchase review emailAuto-triggered 3 days after delivery. More reviews = higher conversion on product pages
  • 4
    Upsell widgets"Complete the look" + "Customers also bought" on product pages. WooCommerce plugin, 15-min setup
  • 5
    Site speed checkImages optimised, lazy loading on. Every 1-second delay = 7% drop in conversions (Google data)
Expected impact: +₹40,000–80,000/month from existing traffic alone
Zara A/B tests every product page element. We apply the same discipline — more revenue from the same visitors, before spending on ads.
Week 3–4 · The Offline Goldmine
Your biggest asset is inside your stores.
  • 1
    WhatsApp broadcast to existing customers"Shop online, get 10% extra." Use phone numbers from billing. Single blast often outperforms 3 months of cold ads
  • 2
    QR code cards in every carry bagAll 25 outlets. Links to site with exclusive online coupon. Print cost under ₹2,000 total
  • 3
    Staff briefing at billing countersMention the website. Small incentive per verified online referral. Converts walk-ins into digital customers
  • 4
    Google Merchant Center setupFree product listings on Google Shopping. Takes 3–5 days approval. Zero cost, immediate visibility
  • 5
    City landing pages"Ethnic wear in [City]" — one per major outlet city. Local SEO that drives both online orders and store footfall
Expected impact: +₹60,000–1,20,000/month from offline base conversion
Reliance Trends uses store footfall as a retargeting list. With 25 stores, Mustard has the same leverage at a fraction of the cost.
Month 2 · Turn on Paid Traffic
Spend only on audiences ready to buy.
  • 1
    Google Shopping ads — ₹300–500/dayFestival + occasion-wear keywords. Ethnic wear ROAS typically 4–8x early on. Scale with data
  • 2
    Instagram Reels — 3/weekAI-scripted, shot in-store or flat lays. Scheduled via Buffer. Reels reach 4x more than static posts
  • 3
    Email list buildingExit-intent popup: 10% off first order. Mailchimp/Klaviyo. Email list is a zero-cost recurring asset
  • 4
    SEO blog launch — 2 posts/month"Best lehenga for wedding season 2025", "Diwali outfit guide by city". AI-drafted, compounds over months
  • 5
    Branded search protection adsCheap, high-intent. Ensure Mustard shows up #1 when someone Googles the brand name
Expected impact: ₹1–1.5L incremental monthly revenue from paid channels
Meesho and Nykaa Fashion pour budget in 6 weeks before each Indian festival peak. We plan campaigns 8 weeks ahead of every major occasion.
Month 3–4 · Retargeting + Seasonal Machine
Warm audiences convert 4–6× cheaper than cold.
  • 1
    Meta catalog retargeting adsShow visitors the exact products they viewed. Pixel now has 60–90 days of data — this is when it gets powerful
  • 2
    Lookalike audiences from top buyersBuild from customers with purchase value > ₹2,000. Meta finds similar profiles across India
  • 3
    Seasonal campaign calendar — 8 weeks aheadNavratri, Dussehra, Diwali, Eid, wedding season, summer. Content + ad budget locked before each peak
  • 4
    City-specific creativesLucknow → chikankari. Jaipur → bandhani. Hyderabad → Kalamkari. Regional relevance lifts CTR significantly
  • 5
    Influencer seeding — 5–10 micro-influencers10K–100K followers, ethnic wear niche, tier 1 + tier 2 cities. Barter or small fee. Trust-based audiences
Expected impact: ₹2–3L/month from paid + organic combined
Fabindia runs city-specific retargeting showing regional styles to regional audiences. A Lucknow customer sees chikankari; a Jaipur customer sees bandhani.
Month 5–6 · Loyalty + Retention Engine
The cheapest customer is one you already have.
  • 1
    Loyalty points programEarn online, redeem online or in-store. Bridges both channels. WooCommerce plugin, seamless setup
  • 2
    Birthday and anniversary campaignsAuto-triggered coupon 7 days before. High open rate, warm sentiment. Collect at email signup
  • 3
    "Refer a friend" program₹200 off for referrer + referee. Tracked via WooCommerce coupon codes. Zero ad cost per acquisition
  • 4
    VIP early access tierCustomers with 2+ purchases get first look at new arrivals. Creates urgency and rewards loyalty
  • 5
    WhatsApp customer communityPrivate group for top buyers — first looks, styling polls, exclusive offers. Highest-engagement channel in India
Target: repeat purchase rate from ~15% → 35%+ over 6 months
Tanishq's loyalty program drives 60%+ revenue from repeat buyers. Manyavar CRM campaigns recover 20–25% of lapsed customers. Same playbook, built for Mustard.

  • 2

  • 3
    YouTube Shorts — styling and occasion guidesLong shelf-life content. "5 ways to style a kurta for Diwali" keeps getting views for years
  • 4
    Vernacular contentHindi + regional language captions and videos for tier 2 city audiences. Massive underserved market in ethnic wear
  • 5
    WhatsApp catalogueProduct catalog directly in WhatsApp for share-to-buy. Huge in India — no app install required
Manyavar, Soch, and W Clothing built online dominance through consistent brand storytelling tied to Indian occasions — not just ads. That's the moat we build here.

How This Gets Done · AI as the Engine
Full visibility into what AI does — and what still needs a human.

✦ Fully AI-handled

  • Product descriptionsAll 500+ SKUs rewritten for SEO — batch processed, reviewed once
  • Blog content"Best lehenga for Diwali 2025" type posts — drafted in minutes, light edit before publish
  • Email sequencesAbandoned cart, post-purchase, birthday — written and set up once, runs forever
  • WhatsApp broadcast copyFestive announcements, new arrivals, sale alerts — tone-matched to brand voice
  • Ad copy variants10–15 headline and description variants per campaign for A/B testing
  • Instagram caption batches4 weeks of captions + hashtag sets generated in one session
  • Monthly analytics reportGA4 data interpreted into plain language — what worked, what didn't, what's next

◈ AI drafts, we refine together

  • Reels scriptsAI writes the hook, structure and CTA — store team shoots, we review for brand fit
  • Seasonal campaign strategyAI generates the brief, angles and copy — we validate timing, offers, business logic
  • Influencer outreach messagesAI drafts personalised pitches — we check tone and relationship context before sending
  • Google Shopping feed optimisationAI rewrites product titles for search intent — we verify category accuracy
  • City landing page contentAI generates local SEO copy for each city — we add store-specific details
  • Customer segmentation logicAI suggests segments from purchase data — we decide which get which campaigns

◉ Human-only

  • Ad spend decisionsWhere to increase budget, what to pause — judgment call based on ROAS data
  • Brand voice sign-offAnything customer-facing gets a final read to ensure it sounds like Mustard
  • Influencer selectionShortlisting, vetting audience quality, negotiating — AI can research but not judge
  • Photography directionWhat to shoot, how to style it — needs a person at the store with the product
  • Pricing + offer decisionsDiscount depth, bundle logic, exclusivity — business calls the brand owns
  • Relationship managementStore staff, partners, influencers, press — trust and context that only humans carry
  • Strategy pivotsWhen something isn't working, the call on what to change requires judgment, not automation
AI handles the volume work — the writing, the variations, the reporting. That's what used to require a 4-person agency team. The strategy, the judgment calls, and the brand decisions are made together. This is how two people can run what an agency charges 10× more to deliver.
